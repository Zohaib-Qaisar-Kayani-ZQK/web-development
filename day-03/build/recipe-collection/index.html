Homemade Margherita Pizza

A simple, classic pizza with fresh basil and mozzarella. This recipe yeilds a thin-crust, flavorful pizza.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza dough ball,
    • Homemade or store-brought.
  • 1/2 cup san marzano tomato sauce,
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced,
  • 1tbsp olive oil,
  • Salt to taste.

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out dough.
  3. Spread sauce.
  4. Top with mozzarella and basil.
  5. Drizzle with oil.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes.

Cooking Time

Total Cooking Time: 25 minutes (15 mins prep, 10 mins cook).
