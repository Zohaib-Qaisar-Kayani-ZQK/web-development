The Beginning: A Message From the Dark

The universe is unimaginably large. Somewhere among its billions of galaxies, countless stars are born, live, and eventually disappear into darkness.

But according to an old story told by astronauts, there is a place beyond the final known star where something is still waiting.

For centuries, explorers searched the sky for signs of intelligent life. Most searches returned nothing more than static, cosmic radiation, and the quiet hum of distant stars.

Then, in the year 2187, scientists detected something different.

It was not a planet. It was not a star. It was not ordinary radio noise.

It was a repeating signal coming from a region of space approximately 14 billion light-years away.

The signal repeated every seventeen minutes.

The greatest mystery of the universe is not how large it is, but why something so vast would leave a light burning for us to find.

— Captain Elias Ward, Expedition Journal

The Last Lighthouse

The signal eventually led scientists to a strange object floating at the boundary of a region known as the Silent Expanse.

From a distance, the object appeared to be a small artificial moon. But as the exploration vessel approached, the crew discovered something impossible.

The object was a lighthouse.

Not a lighthouse designed to guide ships across an ocean, but a structure built to guide travelers across the stars.

Its enormous tower stretched for nearly 30 kilometers, and its central chamber emitted a pale silver light.

The strange thing was that nobody knew who had built it.

There were no markings, no inscriptions, and no records of its construction.

Did You Know?

A light-year is a measurement of distance, not time. It represents the distance light travels in one year.

Light travels approximately 299,792 kilometers per second.

The Forgotten Planet

Behind the lighthouse was a small blue planet. It had no official name, so the explorers called it Orion-7.

At first, Orion-7 looked completely lifeless. Its surface was covered with enormous mountains, frozen oceans, and forests that appeared almost black.

When the explorers landed, they discovered something that changed everything they believed about the planet.

The trees were not actually trees.

They were enormous biological structures that absorbed energy from the planet's atmosphere.

Every structure appeared to point toward the lighthouse.

It was as if the entire planet had been built to watch the light.

The Mysterious Signal

Inside the lighthouse, the crew discovered a circular chamber containing thousands of glowing symbols.

The symbols changed whenever someone approached them.

At first, the scientists believed they were observing a form of alien language.

After several weeks of analysis, however, they discovered that the symbols were not words.

They were memories.

What the Symbols Revealed

  • Images of distant civilizations
  • Maps of unknown galaxies
  • Records of extinct species
  • Warnings about collapsing stars
  • A map pointing toward an unknown region

One symbol appeared repeatedly. It represented a small circle surrounded by seven stars.

The explorers eventually realized that the symbol represented Earth.

Someone had seen us before we ever knew that we were being watched.

— Dr. Mara Singh, Expedition Scientist

The Secret Beneath the Lighthouse

Beneath the main chamber, the explorers discovered a staircase leading deep underground.

The staircase contained exactly 10,000 steps.

At the bottom was a room containing a single object: a small metallic sphere.

It was completely silent.

Then the captain touched it.

The entire room suddenly filled with images.

The crew saw planets being born. They saw stars collapsing. They saw civilizations rising and disappearing.

Finally, they saw Earth.

But the image was not of Earth as it existed in 2187.

It showed Earth millions of years in the future.

The planet was covered in forests again. The oceans were clean. The cities had disappeared.

Humanity was nowhere to be seen.

The Final Journey

The explorers faced an impossible decision.

They could return home with their discovery, or they could follow the map hidden inside the sphere.

The map pointed beyond the edge of their known universe.

The captain chose to continue.

Their spacecraft entered a region where the stars slowly disappeared.

One by one, the instruments stopped working.

Then the final star vanished.

For the first time in human history, a spacecraft was traveling through complete cosmic darkness.

The Last Transmission

Before communication was lost, the crew transmitted one final message to Earth:

We have reached the place beyond the stars. There is no darkness here. There is another sky.

The message ended at exactly 03:17 UTC.

What Lies Beyond?

The expedition never returned.

For years, scientists assumed that the crew had been lost.

But seventeen years later, something unexpected happened.

The lighthouse sent another signal.

This time, the signal contained a message written in a language that humans could understand.

It contained only six words:

We found where the universe begins.

Nobody knows what happened after that.

The lighthouse continues to shine.

Every seventeen minutes.

Waiting.

Watching.

Perhaps one day, someone will answer.

Interesting Facts About the Story

  1. The lighthouse is located beyond the fictional Silent Expanse.
  2. The mysterious signal repeats every seventeen minutes.
  3. Orion-7 is the name given to the unexplored planet.
  4. The lighthouse contains memories rather than traditional written records.
  5. The expedition's final message suggests that the universe may have a beginning that humanity has never imagined.
