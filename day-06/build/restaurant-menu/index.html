Our Story

The Olive Table began with a simple idea: create a restaurant where Mediterranean cooking could meet the warmth of a neighborhood dining room.

Our kitchen is inspired by coastal markets, family recipes, open-fire cooking, seasonal produce, and long dinners shared with friends.

We work with local growers, fish suppliers, bakers, and small producers whenever possible. The menu changes because the market changes.

Good food does not need to be complicated. It needs to be cared for.

Chef Daniel Moretti, Founder

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for dinner, especially Friday and Saturday evenings. Walk-ins are welcome when space is available.

Can I bring a birthday cake?

Outside cakes may be accepted with advance notice. Please contact the restaurant before your visit.

Can menu items be changed?

Small modifications may be possible, but not every dish can be changed because some components are prepared in advance.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Children are welcome during lunch and dinner, and a dedicated children's menu is available.

Does the menu change?

Yes. Seasonal dishes and chef's specials change according to ingredient availability.
